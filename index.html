Editor's letter

§ 01
From the desk of
Joshua Woodyard Now · in London

The best research I've done didn't begin with a brief. It began with someone — a VP, a buyer, a client — saying something like "we don't know why this is happening, and we need to by Tuesday." My job is to sit in that gap between ambiguity and deadline, run the right experiment, and come back with an answer the room can actually use.

My background braids two things. On one side, advanced technical work — R, SQL, dashboards, predictive modelling, ETL pipelines. On the other, behavioural economics and marketing science, studied formally at City and taught informally by six years of watching people make decisions in real buildings.

I work best where the question is fuzzy, the stakes are clear, and the answer has to be legible to both an analyst and a CEO.
